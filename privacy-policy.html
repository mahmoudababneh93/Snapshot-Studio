Snapshot Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

All processing happens on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or stored on our servers. Snapshot Studio has no backend and no account system, and collects no personal information.

What Snapshot Studio does

Snapshot Studio captures a screenshot of the tab you choose — the visible area, a region you drag, or the full page — and lets you download it as a PNG, JPG, WebP, or PDF file. Capturing and saving happen entirely in your browser.

What we collect

Nothing. Snapshot Studio does not send any data off your device — not the images you capture, not your browsing history, and no analytics of page content.

What is stored

Nothing persistent. A capture is held only for the moment it takes to move it from the capture step to the download page (in the browser's local IndexedDB) and is cleared as soon as the download page reads it. It is never uploaded, and no settings, history, or personal data are saved.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • activeTab — capture and read the current tab, and only after you click the toolbar icon or press the capture shortcut. It grants no standing access to your browsing. chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab runs under this grant.
  • scripting — inject Snapshot Studio's on-demand helper (region selection and full-page scroll-and-stitch capture) into the current tab, only when you start a capture.

Snapshot Studio requests no host permissions at install time, so it cannot read or capture any site until you explicitly invoke it. It requests no storage, debugger, or background-access permissions.

Third parties

Snapshot Studio shares no data with any third party, because it collects none.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date changes and the new version ships with the extension update.
